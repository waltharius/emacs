#+title: Test plan
#+startup: overview

Manual tests of the configuration on a live machine, with the real
notes.  Part A covers what touches notes and their folders; a failure
there can lose or misplace writing.  Part B covers appearance and
interface; a failure there is annoying, not harmful.  Tick boxes with
=C-c C-c=; the cookies count progress.  Record anything unexpected under
[[*Findings][Findings]] with the date, the machine and what was done.

Run Part A against a *copy* of the notes (=~/notes2=, section A1), so
that a bug costs a copy.  Where a check reads "on notes2", it means the
root is =~/notes2= at that moment.
